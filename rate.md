1 = Very Low
Technically/presentationally poor enough that you normally
would not want Photo Identity to select it automatically.

2 = Low
Clearly inferior, but still potentially usable.

3 = Normal
Ordinary acceptable archive photo.

4 = High
Clearly better than normal; desirable for presentation.

5 = Very High
Exceptional presentation quality.

6 = Unsure
You genuinely cannot confidently place it.

## P marks Personally important, but that is deliberately independent of quality.
